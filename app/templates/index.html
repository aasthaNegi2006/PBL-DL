Scientific Integrity & Overfitting Protection:
In video sign language recognition, naive random train/test splits cause severe identity leakage when clips of the same individual appear in both training and testing partitions, producing an artificial 100% accuracy. Our system enforces a strict Signer-Independent protocol: 5 signers (80 clips) were completely held out from both training and checkpoint selection. The true, honest generalization accuracy is 97.50% (0.9778 Macro F1), confirming robust spatio-temporal feature generalization to novel signers.
